Projects

keep-mcp

: The most popular open-source Google Keep MCP server by GitHub stars.

human-api

: Can an AI agent find a real person, pay them and get an answer, all on its own? Human API lets agents ask a closed question and pay per answer in USDC over x402.

llm-autobattler

: Two Generals is a medieval autobattler. Buy an army, write your tactics in plain words and watch them fight a friend's. An LLM compiles your orders into a program that runs the whole battle.

split-keyboards

: Ergonomic keyboards for tinkerers. I assembled a Scylla, learned Colemak-DH and customise my QMK and ZMK layouts.

yt-sponsor-detect

: Can you fit a language model into a Chrome extension? A prototype that detects and skips sponsored segments on YouTube, entirely on your device.

heron-deterrent

: Keeps birds away from the fish in my pond. When the garden camera detects an animal, Home Assistant asks Gemini what is in the snapshot. For a bird, a sprinkler valve sprays for 20 seconds and my phone gets a notification. Between 3 and 6 November 2026 it recognised a cat 19 times and kept the water off.

agent-box

: Claude Code and Codex share a container on my home server, and I steer both from my phone through the Claude and ChatGPT apps. When Codex hits its usage limit, a timer resumes the task once usage is available again.

feuer-io

: feuer.io is a multiplayer browser strategy game and my eternal pet project since 2018. I've used it to try different architectures, frameworks and hosting setups.

swift-raytracer

: A raytracer I wrote in Swift in 2021, with an iOS scene editor.

big-pond

: Big Pond is an iOS app for discovering local marine life, built after moving to Taiwan and wondering what was in the water.
