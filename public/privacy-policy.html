Privacy

This notice covers feuer.dev. Last updated 6 September 2026.

Contact

I am Jannik Feuerhahn, responsible for this website. For privacy questions or requests, email jannik@feuer.dev.

Visiting the website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends GitHub the information needed to request and receive pages, including your IP address and request details. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account.

I use this hosting to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis for that necessary processing is my legitimate interest in providing and protecting the website, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub controls its hosting logs and their retention. Its published policy ties retention to the processing purpose and legal obligations, without specifying a fixed retention period for Pages visitor logs. I do not receive those raw visitor logs.

GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. Its privacy statement describes its international transfer safeguards, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.

Cookies, analytics and external content

I do not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or browser storage on this website. Fonts are supplied by your device. Images and scripts are served from this website. Fancy mode is recorded in the page URL, not in a cookie.

Project links and the résumé open external websites. They are links, not embedded content. Their providers process your requests when you follow them.

Email

If you email me, I use your address, message and any attachments to handle your enquiry. This is based on my legitimate interest in responding to correspondence, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. If your enquiry concerns entering into a contract with me, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies instead. Email is transmitted and stored through the email providers involved in the correspondence.

I keep correspondence while it is needed to deal with your enquiry or an ongoing exchange, then delete it unless a legal retention requirement or the establishment, exercise or defence of a legal claim requires keeping it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, and portability where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation. Contact me using the email address above.

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the country where you live or work. The European Data Protection Board lists the authorities.
